<p>We pride ourselves in having published our 2012 and 2015 Bitcoin reports<br> during what we perceived as periods of significant undervaluation. Each<br> report was issued when Bitcoin was down more than 80% from all-time<br> highs. Now, at 75% below its 2017 all-time high, we believe the current bear<br> market represents an exceptional opportunity for value investors.</p>



<p>During this accumulation phase, we expect for Bitcoin to trade in a range<br> of $3,000 to $6,500, until the new bull market permanently cements the<br> denarian cryptocurrency as a multi-trillion dollar asset class.</p>
