<h3>From CoinTelegraph</h3>



<p>Bitcoin (<a href="https://cointelegraph.com/bitcoin-price-index">BTC</a>)
 investors who are waiting for a price dip to even $6,000 have “missed” 
their opportunity already, veteran trader Peter Brandt says.</p>



<p>In a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_dno4YQYa8&amp;feature=youtu.be" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">market discussion</a>
 with Cointelegraph on Jan. 17, the 40-year market stalwart said that 
contrary to what some believe, BTC/USD has already hit its floor.</p>

<h3>Brandt: “The weak hands are out”</h3>



<p>“They
 all now want to sit and buy a break back to $6,000 or $5,000 and 
they’ve missed the bottom — and during that bottom, I think you had a 
lot of people accumulate with strong hands,” he summarized.</p>



<p>Brandt continued:</p>



<blockquote><p>“The weak hands are out; the strong hands own it.”</p></blockquote>



<p>As a long-time Bitcoin advocate, Brandt was continuing a bullish streak he began on social media earlier this month.</p>



<p>As Cointelegraph <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/cryptocultists-may-stop-bitcoin-bull-market-trader-who-called-20k">reported</a>,
 his personal sentiment has undergone a change since late 2019 — as 
recently as December, he had warned there remained a chance for Bitcoin 
to put in lower lows in 2020 thanks to novice investors he described as 
“cryptocultists.”</p>



<p>In early 2018, one month after Bitcoin reached its all-time highs of $20,000, Brandt <a href="https://twitter.com/PeterLBrandt/status/955538177892102144" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">warned</a>
 markets would not be going any higher, and that an 80% retracement was 
likely. BTC/USD hit local lows of $3,100 — 84.5% lower — a year later.</p>



<p>Now, however, the danger has subsided, Brandt suggested, in comments echoed in the discussion by fellow trader Alessio Rastani.</p>



<h3>“Anybody” should have 10-20% BTC portfolio</h3>



<p>“I
 think anybody who is interested in what Bitcoin has to offer has to 
have at least 10-20% of an ownership position relative to the capital 
that they could commit to Bitcoin in a bigger perspective,” he advised.</p>



<p>Bitcoin
 has sealed monthly gains of around 35%, with 2020 progress alone at 
25%. Markets reached local highs of $9,000 on Friday, before 
encountering <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-price-cools-off-at-87k-after-best-january-gains-since-2012">resistance</a>, which coincides with the 200-day moving average price, something which has historically stifled bullish progress.</p>



<p>The
 latest statistics meanwhile suggest that interest in Bitcoin extends 
beyond lay consumers — volume surges on futures markets signal <a href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/cme-bitcoin-futures-see-open-interest-surge-as-global-volume-hits-25b">institutional commitment</a> as well, commentators have said.</p>
