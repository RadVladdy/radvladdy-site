<p><a href="https://twitter.com/search?q=%23Bitcoin">#Bitcoin</a> is a game we play where we can easily tell when someone is cheating.&#8221;<br><br>When I first saw this (in some ELI5 bitcoin Tweet) I thought it just some pithy phrase.<br><br>But over time this simple sentence has come to resonate with me as a profound summary of the bitcoin ethos. </p>



<p>Bitcoin IS a game, but an  *infinite* one, where the goal is to keep playing &#8212; not a finite game  where the goal is to win.<br><br>&#8220;Finite games are theatrical, necessitating an audience; infinite ones are dramatic, involving participants&#8221;<br><br><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_and_Infinite_Games">&nbsp;https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_and_Infinite_Games&nbsp;…</a>                                                                                                                            </p>



<p>Like any game, bitcoin is an opt-in set of rules devised by its players.<br><br>Once the game has begun, changing rules is tough. Only rules which help  players continue playing will survive. And no one is in charge of making  changes.<br><br>So, like the game of culture, Bitcoin evolves.   </p>



<p>Preventing cheating is  important in infinite games because cheaters are seeking to win, not to  continue play.<br><br>Cheating in bitcoin isn&#8217;t prevented by referees (3rd parties) or parents (the state).<br><br>Mathematics, energy &amp; the greed of other players ensures the game continues.                                          </p>



<p>Imagine other games if they were like bitcoin:</p>



<ul><li>A news industry in which those with the incentive to lie don&#8217;t because it&#8217;s more profitable to tell the truth.</li><li>Societies which don&#8217;t pollute because it&#8217;s cheaper to be clean.</li><li>Making people free making people money.                                          </li></ul>



<p> Yes, bitcoin is deadly serious with big fortunes, egos, and ambitions in play.<br><br>But thinking of it as an infinite game can help engender the playfulness required for creativity, innovation, progress.<br><br>To a beautiful game&#8230;                                          </p>
