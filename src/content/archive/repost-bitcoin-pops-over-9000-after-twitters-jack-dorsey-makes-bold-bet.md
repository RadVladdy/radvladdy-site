<p>From Forbes</p>



<p><a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/billybambrough/2020/01/24/bitcoin-and-crypto-could-be-heading-into-a-dicey-weekend/">Bitcoin adoption has stalled and crypto trading volume has crashed</a>—but that’s not stopping&nbsp;<a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/billybambrough/2019/12/01/jack-dorseys-future-defining-plan-to-bring-bitcoin-to-12-billion-people/#fe9859a4f6f3">Twitter’s chief executive Jack Dorsey who’s convinced bitcoin is the future</a>.</p>



<p>The bitcoin price, still half its all-time high,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/billybambrough/2020/01/22/serious-bitcoin-correction-warning-as-greed-sharply-rises/">has struggled to regain the levels it reached in late 2017</a>, though&nbsp;<a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/billybambrough/2020/01/19/forget-the-halvingcould-this-be-what-sends-bitcoin-over-10000/">climbed through last year after a disastrous 2018</a>.</p>



<p>[<em>Updated: 4:53pm EST 27/01/2020</em>] The bitcoin price has soared today, briefly climbing above the $9,000 per bitcoin mark on the Luxembourg-based Bitstamp exchange before falling back slightly. Some have suggested the China coronaviris emergency is behind bitcoin’s 5% rise today,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ft.com/content/165c6a31-3898-47e1-afd0-349a595813e2" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">though others have dismissed those claims</a>.</p>



<p><a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/billybambrough/2019/12/21/a-us-fed-offical-made-a-significant-legal-warning-over-bitcoin-and-crypto/">Amid regulatory scrutiny triggered by social media giant Facebook’s plans to launch its own answer to bitcoin</a>, Dorsey has vowed to help bitcoin development through his payments company, Square—which said it’s “only a matter of time until instant, low-fee bitcoin payments are as common as cash used to be.”</p>

<p>“For bitcoin to become a widely used global currency—one that can’t be stopped, tampered with, or rigged in anyone’s favor—improvements to bitcoin’s [user experience], security, privacy, and scaling are required,” Square’s cryptocurrency division wrote in&nbsp;<a href="https://medium.com/@squarecrypto/what-were-building-lightning-development-kit-1ed58b0cab06" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">a blog post</a>&nbsp;this week.</p>



<p>“Square Crypto is focusing on Lightning,” Dorsey added via&nbsp;<a href="https://twitter.com/jack/status/1219774343988690944" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Twitter</a>.</p>



<p>Square Crypto, which was created last year by Dorsey to spearhead bitcoin and cryptocurrency development at the company, has said it’s building a development kit for bitcoin’s Lightning network, a way for smaller bitcoin payments to be sent more quickly and cheaply without clogging up the bitcoin blockchain.</p>



<p>“Because we are a consolidated team of open-source developers, we are in a strong position to coordinate on major projects that individuals may not have the time or resources to tackle,” Square Crypto added.</p>



<p>Bitcoin’s Lightning network has been praised by some as the potential saviour of bitcoin transactions—something that will allow the clunky and encumbered original blockchain-based cryptocurrency to compete with the likes of more nimble dash, litecoin, and Ripple’s XRP.</p>



<p>However,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/billybambrough/2018/07/06/bitcoins-lightning-network-could-play-havoc-with-the-bitcoin-price/#3adbe3b77f1a">it’s unclear exactly what mainstream adoption of the bitcoin Lightning network would mean for the bitcoin price</a>.</p>



<p>Research carried out by bitcoin and cryptocurrency exchange Bitmex this month&nbsp;<a href="https://blog.bitmex.com/lightning-network-part-6-over-60000-non-cooperative-channel-closures/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">found</a>&nbsp;that development of bitcoin’s Lightning network “is still for hobbyists and technologists,” but revealed usage was ahead of expectations.</p>



<p>Meanwhile, Dorsey’s Square last week was awarded a U.S. patent for a network that enables crypto-to-fiat transactions in real-time.</p>
